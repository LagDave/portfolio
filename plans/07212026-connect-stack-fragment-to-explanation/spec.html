Connect the selected stack fragment to its explanation

Status
In Progress

Size

Risk
Level 2

Why

The framed background makes the stack illustration feel like a card inside the section, while the explanation card has no clear visual relationship to the fragment that controls it.

What

Remove the stack illustration's inner framed/grid background and draw a responsive animated connector from the hovered, focused, or selected fragment to the explanation card. The flow animation must stop for users who prefer reduced motion.

Assumption: “pyramid background” means the large white bordered panel and its nested grid behind the black fragments, not the section-level blueprint grid.

Context

Relevant Files

  • src/components/Technologies.tsx — owns stack fragment selection and the explanation card.
  • src/hooks/useElementConnector.ts — new geometry hook for the relationship between live DOM elements.

Patterns To Follow

  • Use the existing local hook shape in src/hooks/useMousePosition.ts: frame-scheduled browser events with complete cleanup.
  • Use the installed Framer Motion dependency for motion and its reduced-motion preference.
  • Apply Code Constitution §1.2, §2.1, §2.2, §4.2, §13.2, §17.2, and §20.3. The Alloro-specific API triad in §12.1 does not apply to this standalone portfolio.

Reference file: src/hooks/useMousePosition.ts — closest analog for requestAnimationFrame scheduling and event cleanup.

Constraints

Must

  • Preserve hover, focus, pointer, and click selection.
  • Recalculate connector geometry when layout or sticky positioning changes.
  • Keep the connector decorative and non-interactive.
  • Honor prefers-reduced-motion.

Must Not

  • Do not add a dependency.
  • Do not touch unrelated dirty files.
  • Do not use fixed connector coordinates.

Out Of Scope

  • Changing stack copy, fragment geometry, or explanation content.
  • Drawing the decorative connector when the explanation card stacks below the fragments on small screens.

Risk

Level: 2 — responsive geometry and sticky positioning can make a fixed SVG connector drift.

Risks Identified

  • Connector endpoints can become stale after resize, font/layout changes, or sticky-card movement. Mitigation: measure the actual source, target, and container bounds with frame scheduling, resize observation, scroll updates, and desktop media-query gating.
  • Continuous dash motion can harm accessibility. Mitigation: render a static connector under reduced motion.

Blast radius: the Technologies section only; consumers are src/App.tsx and visitors interacting with that section.

Pushback

  • A fixed CSS line would be simpler, but it would not stay attached to fragments of different widths. Live measurement is the recommended path.
